## Description (paste as plain text; use itch's Bold button if you want bold)

There are two worlds: PINK and CYAN. Every enemy and every bullet lives in one of them.

You can only hurt, and be hurt by, things from the world you are standing in. Enemies from the other world are harmless ghosts.

Press SHIFT to swap worlds. Dodge a swarm. Escape a bullet storm. Flip into the other world to fight whatever lives there.

Features
- Your gun aims and fires by itself. Your only real weapon is knowing when to SHIFT.
- Shifting blasts enemies of the world you enter and deletes their bullets.
- Phase kills (kill soon after a shift) score double.
- Splitters break into minis from the OPPOSITE world.
- From wave 3 reality gets unstable and shifts by itself.
- THE GLITCH, a boss that flips between worlds while firing rings of orbs.
- 13 upgrades, including Afterimage, Time Slip, Orbit Shards and Double Vision.
- Two music layers, one per world. The world you are not in keeps playing, muffled, underneath.

Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (drag on the left side on a phone)
- Shift worlds: Space, Shift, E, F or click (tap the right side on a phone)
- Pause: P. Mute: M.
